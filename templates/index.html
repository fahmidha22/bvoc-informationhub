Message from the Director

Welcome to the MES School of B.Voc Studies.

As we navigate an era of rapid technological advancement, the bridge between formal education and industry requirements has never been more critical. Our B.Voc programs are meticulously designed to move beyond traditional rote learning, focusing instead on competency-based skill development and hands-on technical expertise.

At MES, we are committed to nurturing “industry-ready” professionals. By integrating rigorous academic curriculum with intensive On-the-Job Training (OJT), we ensure our students are not just graduates, but specialists equipped to excel in leading sectors ranging from Industrial Automation to Digital Media and Animation.

Our mission is simple: to empower our students with the skills of tomorrow, the ethics of a professional, and the vision of an innovator. We invite you to be part of this transformative journey.

Dr. Manoj
Director, B.Voc
MES College, Marampally
